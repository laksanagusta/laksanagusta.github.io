Inline links keep a subtle underline at rest. Social links use a 12px / 18px, 1px 7px, 999px pill with 6% ink background and 80% ink text, increasing to 10% on hover. External links and contact channels open in a new tab. All links use a subtle Hover effect with a Scale of 1.015 over 150ms ease-out. The button focus ring stays pill-shaped. The contact action opens a channel chooser with layered shadow depth, a 32px hover circle, and an optically centered 16px close icon inside a 40px hit area. The modal keeps only a 14px title above the contact options.

Layout and spacing

552px reading column · 118px desktop top margin · 24px mobile gutters · 64px mobile top margin. Identity to bio: 36px. Bio to writing: 64px. Writing label to title: 16px. Writing to contact: 56px. Contact paragraphs use justified alignment. Contact paragraph to action: 20px. Action to footer: 116px, or 88px on mobile. The contact chooser stays within the viewport, scrolls inside its card along the block axis when needed, and wraps long channel values.
