# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineering managers, founders, and collaborators who need to quickly understand Dika Laksana's engineering range, project taste, and fit for backend-heavy product work. They arrive with limited time and want proof of shipped systems, not a decorative resume.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Dika Laksana as a software engineer who builds practical operational systems across risk governance, fleet and field operations, ecommerce, and AI-assisted workflows. Success means a visitor can understand the positioning, inspect credible project evidence, review work history, and start a focused conversation.

## Brand Personality

Premium, minimalist, technical. The interface should feel calm, precise, and intentionally restrained while still carrying enough visual confidence to avoid feeling like a generic resume page.

## Anti-references

Avoid AI-template portfolio layouts, overly corporate resume sites, and flashy creative agency styling. The site should not feel like a generated landing page, a LinkedIn clone, or a decorative agency pitch.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with proof, not adjectives.
2. Keep the surface quiet so the project evidence carries the weight.
3. Use restraint deliberately, not as a lack of visual direction.
4. Make contact easy without turning the page into a sales funnel.
5. Preserve a technical, credible tone across copy, spacing, and interaction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for all text and interactive states. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Keep navigation, contact actions, project links, and external links keyboard-accessible with clear labels.
